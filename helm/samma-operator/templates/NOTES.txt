Upgrades: `helm upgrade` does not change CustomResourceDefinitions. After an
upgrade that ships a CRD change, apply them from the chart:
  helm pull oci://ghcr.io/samma-io/charts/samma-operator --version {{ .Chart.Version }} --untar
  kubectl apply -f samma-operator/crds/
